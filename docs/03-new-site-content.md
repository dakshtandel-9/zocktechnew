## 7. FAQ

**Eyebrow:** FAQ
**Heading:** Questions, answered.
**Side card:** **Still have questions?** Message us on WhatsApp. We usually reply within `[1 hour]`. → Chat on WhatsApp

1. **How much does a project cost?**
   Every project is custom-quoted, because no two businesses need the same thing. Tell us what you need on WhatsApp or through the form and we'll send a clear, fixed quote, usually within 24 hours. No hidden costs, no surprise invoices.

2. **How fast can you deliver?**
   Landing pages in 3–5 days, business websites in 5–7 days, and e-commerce stores in 10–15 days. Marketing and content are ongoing, and your first campaign or posts go live within `[X]` days. Timelines start after the advance payment and kickoff.

3. **Can I hire you for just one service?**
   Yes. Many clients start with a website and add ads or Instagram later. Pick what you need now and we'll grow with you.

4. **What do you need from me to start?**
   Your business name, logo, colour preferences and any content you already have. We guide you through everything else.

5. **How does payment work?**
   For projects, 50% advance to start and 50% on delivery and approval. For monthly services like ads and Instagram management, billing is `[monthly in advance]`. UPI and bank transfer accepted.

6. **Do you offer revisions?**
   Yes. Every quote includes revision rounds, and we agree on the number upfront so there are no surprises.

7. **Can you handle the domain, hosting and maintenance?**
   Yes. We can set up and manage your domain and hosting, or help you run it yourself. Ongoing updates and new pages can be added any time after launch.

8. **Will my website work on mobile?**
   Every site we build is mobile-first and tested on multiple screen sizes before delivery.

*Optional extras:*
- **What tech do you use?** Mostly React and Next.js, which are fast, SEO-friendly and easy to maintain. Simple landing pages can also be pure HTML/CSS/JS. `[Confirm: still no WordPress or Shopify?]`
- **Do you work with creators?** Yes. We edit reels, write scripts and manage Instagram pages for creators and personal brands.
